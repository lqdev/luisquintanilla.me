Here's something I didn't have on my bingo card for this year. Building apps for Garmin wearable devices. Using a combination of Opus 4.8 and MAI Code 1 Flash, I've been able to put together some nice utilities that leverage the [Garmin Fenix 6X](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/641435/) capabilities and sensors. After having this device for several years now, I love it. Being able to build my own apps for it has given it new life and made it even better.
