That means that notes like these are posted as [records](https://pdsls.dev/at://did:plc:pme7qquljcdx6i4zyawoxypd/app.bsky.feed.post/3mql6jgoavwtp) which [you can see on Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/lqdev.me/post/3mql6jgoavwtp) and my blog posts adopted the [standard.site lexicon](https://standard.site/) and can be viewed on [platforms which support it (including Bluesky)](https://bsky.social/about/blog/06-22-2026-summer-of-standard-site).
