> The future of social media is websites.

I completely agree. 

SocialSpec is a really cool project from [Caleb Hailey](https://calebhailey.com/links/socialspec/).

It's nice to see [Manton](https://www.manton.org) introduce [support for it in Micro.blog](https://www.manton.org/2026/10/13/i-added-a-new-plugin.html)

I'd already supported several of the components like vCard, RSS feeds, and following (blogrolls) on my website. However, building a standard that builds on those as the foundation rather than reinventing the wheel makes a ton of sense. Given I already have these components, adding support for the spec to my site should be trivial. 

I'm excited to see where this project goes.
