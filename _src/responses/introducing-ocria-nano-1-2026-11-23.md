> Today, we’re releasing the open weights for OCRia-nano-1, an extremely lightweight 8.1M-parameter model that brings fast, accurate document parsing to your local CPUs. We think it should be the default first pass for all document collections within your organization, even if the hardest documents require larger VLMs. And, we are releasing the full open weights on Hugging Face!
