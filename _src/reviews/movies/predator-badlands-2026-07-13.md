Although I'm not a die-hard fan of the Predator franchise, I've watched the original [Predator](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/106-predator) and [Prey](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/766507-prey). I noticed the Prey director had made a new entry in Predator: Badlands and given I enjoyed Prey, I was excited to check it out.

I liked the subtle nods and references to the Alien universe. There were a few moments where you could see parallels to the Marvel movies like Guardians of the Galaxy. From reviews I've read, this is something that fans of the Predator franchise had issues with. Personally though, I thought it was fun and a different take on Predator.

I would say the film takes a while to set up and while there are things happening throughout the first hour, a lot of it is setting you up for the final arc which I really enjoyed. The last 30-45 minutes of the film were so good and I'm excited to see which direction the next entry goes.
